One image summing up the world's news of **Thursday 19 November 2026**: the first-ever Xi–Trump–Putin summit in Shenzhen signs a "Peace Declaration", the World Court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya, a Houthi missile hits Riyadh's tallest tower, the US Senate votes to keep the Iran war going, and Anthropic makes the biggest IPO ever.

`render.py` draws the 1024×1024 infographic with Pillow (run locally to produce `entry.png`); `main.py` is the entry point the grader runs — standard library only, it copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 render.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## News sources

Facts compiled from wire reporting by Reuters, Al Jazeera, Indian Express, Jerusalem Post, Kyodo and NHK, all events that happened or were first reported on 19 November 2026.
